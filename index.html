Provably fair — how the tide decides

In ⚓ Tide mode your bet names a Bitcoin (testnet4) block that does not exist yet. When that block is mined, the race seed is sha256(blockHash | mark) — your mark is a random nonce fixed at bet time. The seed picks a number on a 10,000-point line; each boat owns a band of it sized by its odds. Nobody — not you, not this page, not anyone — knows the winner before the tide comes in, and anyone can recompute it afterwards from public chain data. The verify ✓ in the log does exactly that, in your browser.

Even the race replay is honest: every gust and stall is drawn from the same seed, so two replays of the same race are identical, anywhere.

Practice mode is seeded locally and marked as such — instant, but not provable.

Doubloons are play money. Part of tide-games — source on GitHub · maths in regatta.js, 100% pure and testable · house edge 3%, identical for every boat.
